# Pilot Training — Cashier

## Goals

- Complete lookup, earn, and redeem flows consistently.
- Capture request IDs for support escalation.
- Avoid unsafe manual workarounds.

## Required skills

1. Start from an assigned device only.
2. Confirm customer/card details before checkout.
3. Recognize success, retryable failure, and escalation-only failure states.
4. Escalate duplicate-credit, lost-card, SMS, or offline-sync issues with request IDs.

## Prohibited actions

- Do not promise manual balance fixes.
- Do not reuse receipts or card identifiers across customers.
- Do not bypass approval or support flows.

## Completion evidence

- Supervised synthetic lookup completed
- Supervised earn completed
- Supervised redeem completed
- Cashier acknowledges escalation paths and prohibited actions
